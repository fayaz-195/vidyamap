Meet the Founder

Shaik Mohammad Fayaz

Founder of VidyaMap | Professional Graphic Designer

Shaik Mohammad Fayaz is the founder of VidyaMap and a professional graphic designer with an interest in creative visual communication, brand identity, and digital promotion. Through VidyaMap, he aims to make education-related information easier to discover while helping educational institutions present themselves clearly to students and families.

His creative approach brings together design thinking, accessible information, and digital-first communication. The goal is to build a platform where students can explore educational options and colleges can communicate their offerings in a more engaging way.

Graphic DesignBrand IdentityCreative CommunicationDigital PromotionEducation Discovery

Vision for VidyaMap

To make the journey of discovering colleges and educational opportunities simpler for students, while giving institutions a clear and creative way to reach their audience. VidyaMap is being developed around discoverability, useful information, and thoughtful digital presentation.
