Before you apply

Read the official notification PDF. Check age on the cut-off date, qualification, category relaxations, attempts, physical standards and final-year rules.

Keep documents ready

Recent photo, signature, ID proof, 10th certificate, marksheets, category/EWS/PwBD certificate if applicable, domicile and a working email/mobile.

Apply safely

Use only the official portal linked here. Never pay agents, share OTP/passwords or trust WhatsApp job offers. Save your application and payment receipt.

Eligibility summaries are general guidance, not a substitute for the current notification. Application fees, age limits, exam pattern, required certificates and selection stages can change.
